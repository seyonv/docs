Difficulty in Base Fibonacci Division controls the range of random factors the game picks when it generates a new puzzle. Those factors determine how large the dividend **N** is, which in turn determines how many Fibonacci tiles appear on the board and how spread-out the target slots are. A higher difficulty means a bigger number, a larger grid, and a longer road to the solution.

## Difficulty Levels Compared

| Level | Factor range | Typical grid size | Good for |
|-------|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Easy** | 2 – 5 | Small (≤ 4 × 4) | Newcomers learning the four moves |
| **Medium** *(default)* | 4 – 8 | Medium | Balanced challenge once you know the basics |
| **Hard** | 6 – 13 | Larger (6 × 6+) | Experienced players seeking a deeper puzzle |

## How Difficulty Affects Gameplay

Each factor is represented on the board as its [Zeckendorf (base-Fibonacci) decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeckendorf%27s_theorem). Larger factors have longer Zeckendorf representations, which means:

- **More tiles** are placed on the board at the start.
- **More legal moves** are available at any given moment, creating a wider search space.
- **More steps** are needed to reach the target arrangement, so the optimal move count grows significantly.
- The built-in solver works harder and may occasionally time out on the most complex Hard boards.

On Easy, puzzles typically resolve in just a handful of moves. On Hard, even the optimal solution can require a dozen or more carefully chosen steps.

## Changing Difficulty

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to the title screen">
    Press **Esc** or click the **Menu** button in the bottom button bar to go back to the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a difficulty">
    Click the **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard** button on the title screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new puzzle">
    Click **Play** to generate a fresh puzzle at your chosen difficulty level.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start on **Easy** to build an intuition for the four moves and how tiles relate to each other. Move up to **Medium** once you can complete a puzzle without reaching for the Hint button.
</Tip>
